## How Do I Vet a Furnace Repair Company in Bakersfield? (CSLB License, C-20)

California requires anyone who repairs or installs a furnace for pay to hold a C-20 warm-air heating, ventilating, and air conditioning contractor license issued by the Contractors State License Board, or to work directly under a licensed qualifier. Before hiring any company on this list, or any company at all, pull up the [CSLB's public license search](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/) and confirm the license is active and the classification covers HVAC work.

Ask every company for its license number up front, and check it yourself rather than taking "licensed and insured" at face value on a website or a truck door. A company that hesitates to give you a license number is a red flag regardless of its star rating. All Pro's CSLB license number is 960566; verify it the same way you'd verify any contractor's before signing a repair agreement.

It also helps to ask what AFUE rating a replacement furnace carries if repair isn't worth it anymore. Units that carry the [ENERGY STAR label](https://www.energystar.gov/products/furnaces) typically run at 90% AFUE or higher, which matters in a Bakersfield winter even though the furnace season here is short.
