## Why Maricopa Properties See Plumbing Emergencies

Maricopa grew up as an oil-field town, and a good share of the housing stock dates back to that era. That means galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drain pipe are still doing work in homes that are well past the age those materials were meant to last. Add in the valley's extreme heat swings, where ground temperatures bake hard in summer and contract again in winter, and you get soil movement that stresses buried lines at the joints. Many properties outside the town core also rely on private wells and septic systems rather than municipal hookups, and well water in this part of Kern County tends to run hard. Mineral buildup inside pipes and water heater tanks is a common cause of the sudden failures that bring people to search for a plumber at 2 a.m.
