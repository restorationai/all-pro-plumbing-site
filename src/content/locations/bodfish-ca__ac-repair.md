## Why Bodfish Properties See AC Repair Issues

A lot of the housing stock around Bodfish is manufactured or mobile homes set up in the decades when the Kern River Valley was mostly a weekend and retirement community, and those units often carry package AC systems mounted on the roof or a small pad that were sized for a smaller footprint than what's actually being cooled today. Add in the dust that blows off the surrounding hillsides during dry months, and condenser coils here clog faster than they would in a cleaner suburban environment. Older homes also tend to run alongside an evaporative cooler that was installed before central air went in, and the two systems sometimes fight each other if the ductwork wasn't properly isolated.

## Our Air Conditioning Repair Process in Bodfish

We start by checking static pressure and airflow before touching refrigerant, because a dirty coil or a collapsed flex duct run under a mobile home will mimic a refrigerant problem and lead to an unnecessary recharge. If the system is genuinely low on refrigerant, we pressure test the line set and coils to find the actual leak point rather than just topping it off, since a top-off on a system with an active leak only buys a few weeks before the same call comes back. For package units on manufactured homes, we also inspect the roof curb and flashing, since that's a common spot for refrigerant line chafing in this area. Once the repair is made, we verify superheat and subcooling against manufacturer spec and check supply air temperature at the farthest register in the house, not just at the unit.
